Two things worth knowing before you decide.

14 days to raise anything

You have 14 days from delivery to raise any production error — wrong density, colour inconsistency, base construction fault. If it’s on us, we repair or replace at no cost.

First systems are rarely perfect

That’s expected. Most customers adjust density or grey blend on their second order. By the third, the spec is locked and it simply repeats. We build that iteration into how we work.
