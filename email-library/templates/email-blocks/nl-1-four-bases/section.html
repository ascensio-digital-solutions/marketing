The four bases

What each one costs you.

Lace (Swiss, French)

Most natural hairline, breathable, scalp shows through the mesh.

3–6 months

Skin (Nano 0.03mm → Dura 0.12mm)

Clean shaved-scalp illusion, easiest to attach and remove.

1–8 months

Mono (Mono Pro)

Most durable, natural movement, comfortable for daily wear.

5–8 months

Hybrid (Lace Edge, Mono Fusion LF, Thin Skin LF)

Lace hairline with a PU perimeter for secure bonding.

3–6 months
 

A rough rule that holds up: if you’re new and nervous about detection, start lace at the front. If you’re active, sweat a lot, or want the simplest routine, start skin or hybrid. If you want maximum life per unit, Mono Pro.
