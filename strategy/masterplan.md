# Masterplan summary

Shopify email is a retention-first system for opt-in nurture, behavioral recovery, post-purchase education, replenishment, and win-back.

Initial priority order:

1. Welcome Series
2. Abandoned Cart
3. Abandoned Checkout
4. Post-Purchase Education
5. Browse Recovery
6. Reorder Check-In
7. Win-Back

Every implementation must have an audience, trigger, delay logic, sequence objective, primary CTA, suppression rule, exit condition, KPI, and QA evidence.
