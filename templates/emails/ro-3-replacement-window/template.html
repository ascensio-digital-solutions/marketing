Lead time

A replacement takes 8–10 weeks to make. If you order the day your current one fails, you have a gap with no system. Most people order their second while the first still has a few weeks left, and rotate between them.

Rotation

Rotating two systems roughly doubles the life of both. Neither is worn continuously, adhesive gets a full cure between wears, and the bases last considerably longer.
